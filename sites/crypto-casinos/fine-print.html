the US workaround

How sweepstakes casinos work

step 1

Buy Gold Coins

You pay real money for a "Gold Coin" pack. Gold Coins have no cash value: legally you bought entertainment.

step 2

Get free Sweeps Coins

Each pack comes with a "free" bonus of Sweeps Coins (Stake Cash on Stake.us). No purchase is necessary to get them — a mail-in request also works — which is what keeps it a sweepstakes, not a lottery.

step 3

Play casino games

Sweeps Coins are wagered on the same slots and originals as the real casino.

step 4

Redeem winnings

Sweeps Coins won can be redeemed for cash or crypto at roughly 1:1 after KYC. That redemption is what states now call illegal gambling.

What to take from this

Write terms you'd defend publicly

Vague confiscation and "irregular play" clauses save money once and cost reputation for good. BC.Game's old operators were declared bankrupt over $2M+ unpaid.

Geofence before signup

Block restricted regions at the edge and in-app, then enforce at signup. Taking deposits and confiscating later is the worst of both.

Budget for compliance

Curaçao LOK is ~€47k a year plus per-check KYC, and regulators are tightening: the UK exit, US sweepstakes bans, ACMA blocks.
