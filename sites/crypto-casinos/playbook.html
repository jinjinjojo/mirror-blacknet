The wagering requirement is the real price of a bonus. At 40x on 4% slots, a $100 bonus forces $4,000 of bets, which the house expects to win $160 on: a $60 average gain after paying the bonus. Drop the requirement, allow low-edge games or remove the cap and it flips negative, which is exactly what bonus hunters look for. Contribution rates and max cash-outs are how operators keep the math on their side.

Every lesson in one place

OVERVIEW
The edge is the business

Rewards, affiliates, bonuses and marketing are all priced as a share of house edge × volume. Model that first; the site is a wrapper around it.

Own the margin, rent the rest

The big operators build the wallet, originals and rewards engine and rent slots, KYC, sportsbook and support. Start rented, bring pieces in-house as volume grows.

Trust is the moat

The failures in this dossier (unpaid winnings, hot-wallet hacks, regulator exits) are trust failures. Fast payouts and published odds are features.

ARCH
Pick boring infrastructure

All five big sites sit behind Cloudflare on a mainstream framework (SvelteKit, React, SolidJS, Next). The edge does WAF, bot checks and geofencing before your app sees a request.

Design the event stream early

Live bets, chat and balances run on GraphQL subscriptions, socket.io or Centrifugo. Social proof is part of the product, so realtime is part of the architecture.

Put vendors behind adapters

Keep the ledger, game logic and rewards behind your own API and wrap KYC, aggregators and payments so they can be swapped. Stake runs both Veriff and Sumsub.

OPERATORS
Your bundle is public

Feature-flag names like FIREBLOCKS_INTEGRATION, vendor keys, 112–350 GraphQL operation names and even git hashes were readable in shipped JavaScript. Audit what your build exposes.

Fingerprints reveal your platform

Shared API paths and payment IDs showed BitStarz and 7Bit run on the same white label. If you rent, your differentiation has to come from product, not the skin.

The vendor map is a build plan

Every stack converges on the same categories: aggregator, KYC, fraud, CRM, on-ramp, gateway, custody. Use the operator pages as a shopping list.

HOUSE EDGE
EV = Σ p × payout − 1

Set win chance and payout so EV equals −edge. A 2x game at 1% edge wins 49.5%. For any multiplier m: win chance = (1 − edge) / m.

Volume, not luck

The edge only shows up over many bets; any single session is noise. Bet speed, auto-bet and retention matter more to revenue than any one outcome.

RTP is a config value

Slot studios ship 96 / 94 / 88% variants and the operator picks. If you build games, make RTP a setting and show it honestly.

MONEY FLOW
Model GGR → NGR → net first

A white label at $10M monthly wagers keeps ~6% of GGR and needs ~$7.5M a month just to cover fixed costs. Know your break-even before paying for a platform.

Cap your downside

One $300k winner turns that month red. Size max bet and max win to your bankroll, not to your marketing.

Margin lives in-house

Originals carry no provider fee and an owned platform drops the 10–30% rev share. Together they roughly double margin, which is the roadmap from rented to owned.

REVENUE
Size markets by GGR, not deposits

Deposits churn; revenue is very roughly 15–25% of deposits at these edges. Competitor deposit charts overstate the business by 4–6×.

Scale compounds

Stake's tech parent ran a 26% net margin on A$970M. Vendor terms and fixed costs shrink per dollar as volume grows, so early unit economics look worse than late ones.

Revenue-share tokens are a commitment

RLB, SHFL and BC tie a token to casino revenue. Good marketing, but it locks a fixed share of revenue and draws regulatory attention.

PAYOUTS
Size the hot wallet

Keep about 1–2 days of withdrawals hot and the rest in multisig cold storage. Stake lost $41M from hot wallets; the cold split capped the damage.

Offer cheap, fast chains

Roobet runs 10 chains with 1–32 confirmations. SOL, TRX and LTC make withdrawals feel instant, which is the moment players judge you.

Verify before payout day

KYC thresholds trade signup friction against withdrawal disputes. Verifying early moves the friction to a calm moment instead of a big win.

MARKETING
Price rewards off the edge

Rakeback as a % of edge × wager can never exceed margin. Stake's 5% of edge returns $0.15 per $100 at a 3% edge.

Net affiliate pay against losses

Negative carryover means you never pay commission on money you lost. Dropping it is a cost of competing for affiliates, so price it in.

Distribution is a line item

Stake's growth came from streamers, its own streaming site and sports sponsorships. Budget distribution next to build cost for any consumer product.

FINE PRINT
Write terms you'd defend publicly

Vague confiscation and "irregular play" clauses save money once and cost reputation for good. BC.Game's old operators were declared bankrupt over $2M+ unpaid.

Geofence before signup

Block restricted regions at the edge and in-app, then enforce at signup. Taking deposits and confiscating later is the worst of both.

Budget for compliance

Curaçao LOK is ~€47k a year plus per-check KYC, and regulators are tightening: the UK exit, US sweepstakes bans, ACMA blocks.

METHOD
Anyone can do this to your site

Everything here came from public pages and shipped JavaScript. Run the same bundle review on your own site before someone else does.

Label your evidence

Tag numbers as observed, reported, estimate or allegation. It keeps your own analysis honest and tells readers what to trust.

Blocking isn't hiding

Cloudflare stopped automated browsers on 5 sites, but their licences, companies and economics were still public.

What to take from this

Ship the math first

Before any UI: payout tables with EV = −edge, a P&L model, and max-win limits sized to bankroll.

Rent, measure, then build

Launch on rented parts, find what volume flows through, and build in-house where the fees are largest.

Publish your odds

Showing edge and RTP per game costs nothing and is the cheapest trust signal you can ship.
