The most active sandwich bot on Ethereum. EigenPhi measured $40.6M revenue but only $6.3M profit in its first three months (to May 2023), with close to $7M spent on gas in two months. By 2025 it was linked to roughly 70% of all Ethereum sandwiches, while net sandwich profit across the whole market averaged about $260K a month.

We pulled its deployed bytecode with eth_getCode, ran the same EVMole 0.9.3 engine REA uses (REA's EVM provider only runs on Linux), disassembled the entry path, and decoded two of its sandwiches from 18 Oct 2026 transaction by transaction.

No ABI, on purpose

Normal contracts start by reading a 4-byte function selector (PUSH1 0xe0 SHR) and matching it against a table. EVMole found 0 selectors and the dispatcher pattern is absent. Instead, 335 CALLDATALOADs and 144 SHRs unpack a custom, tightly packed calldata format. Fewer calldata bytes means less gas, and in sandwiching gas is the whole margin.

entry code

The front door: owner-only, one-second window

CALLVALUE PUSH1 0xff AND; low byte of msg.value
TIMESTAMP PUSH1 0xff AND; low byte of block.timestamp
EQ; must match: tx only works in the block it was planned for
PUSH20 0xae2fc483…1fae13 CALLER EQ; sender must be jared's wallet
AND PUSH1 0x28 JUMPI; both true → continue
PUSH2 0x3476 JUMP; otherwise → exit early, burning almost no gas
JUMPDEST PUSH0 CALLDATALOAD PUSH0 BYTE; read the first calldata byte (the 'command')
PUSH1 0x06 SHL JUMP; jump to byte × 64: a 256-slot handler table

Verified on its three most recent transactions. The odd wei amounts it sends are a timing lock: a sandwich only makes sense in the block where the victim lands. If it slips into another block, the first fifteen instructions reject it and almost no gas is burned. After that, the first calldata byte is shifted left by 6 and used as a jump target: 256 command handlers spaced 64 bytes apart. Every sandwich we saw used command 0x2b.

05
where the money went

The builder wins the sandwich

Bid away to win

Both sandwiches netted within about 108,000 wei of zero. The exit transaction's priority fee is sized to hand over almost the whole profit, because rival bots bid for the same slot and the highest bid wins.

Same builder, both blocks

Both blocks paid fees to 0x4838B106…5f97, publicly labelled Titan Builder. Builders assemble blocks from competing bundles and keep the bids. Why jared runs at near-zero margin on these trades isn't visible on-chain.

What this means for you

The victim still paid the inflated price. Sending swaps through a private or MEV-protected RPC and keeping slippage tight removes the opening the bot needs. Copying jared isn't a business: the profit is already competed away.
