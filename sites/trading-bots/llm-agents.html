04
why it failed

Fees, leverage, and a coin-flip win rate

Fees doubled a loss

One run (gpt-5.1 · competition 1) reported -1,366 before fees and -2,726 after, with 906 in fees paid. The API's fee fields don't reconcile exactly, but the direction is clear: trading every few minutes is expensive.

Leverage magnified noise

Average leverage of 6–13x with 26–41% win rates means a few bad calls do lasting damage. In Season 1 the spread between best (+22%) and worst (−63%) came from the same prompt and market.

An LLM is a reader, not an edge

The models saw the same indicators as everyone else at minute resolution, against firms reacting in microseconds. Alpha Arena is free to watch; for nof1 it works as a benchmark and marketing for its own models.
