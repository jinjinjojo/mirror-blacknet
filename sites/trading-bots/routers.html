telegram bot routers

Every bot trade passes through their contract

Telegram trading bots like Maestro and Banana Gun don't trade from your wallet directly. They send your swap through their own router contract on Ethereum, which is where the fee is taken. 02
what the code says

An adjustable toll on an upgradeable road

The fee is a setting

Maestro's changeFee(uint16) stores the rate as a 16-bit number, the natural size for basis points (100 = 1%). setCollector(address) picks the wallet it flows to. Reviews list Maestro at about 1% per trade and Banana Gun at 0.5% manual / 1% snipes.

The code can change

Both routers are upgradeable proxies. The address you approve tokens to stays the same while the owner can swap the logic behind it at any time. That's normal for fast-moving products, and it means you're trusting the operator, not the code you read today.

Paid either way

The router takes its cut on the buy and again on the sell, whether the trade wins or loses. Run the numbers below with your own trading pattern.
