pro firm · Execution

Co-located servers, FPGA order entry, microwave links, smart routing to cut market impact. Tick-to-trade in microseconds.

retail bot · Execution

Home internet or a cloud VPS calling a REST API: 50–300 ms. Market orders pay the full spread plus slippage. Memecoin terminals fan one transaction out to ~7 relays to land faster.
