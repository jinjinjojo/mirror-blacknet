01
the hope business

The most reliable bot profit is selling the bot

Real trading bots exist and some make fortunes, but almost none are for sale. Medallion stopped taking outside money once it worked. What gets marketed to the public sits on a spectrum: honest tools that mostly earn from fees, hype that sells hope, and outright fraud. These are documented cases and the patterns behind them.

03
documented patterns

8 ways bots are used to take money

S1//The bot that never existed
the pitch

Mirror Trading International promised an AI bot trading bitcoin and forex with steady returns and a referral ladder.

what was actually happening

The CFTC alleged there was no trading bot and no profitable trading. Account statements came from demo accounts. A South African court called it a Ponzi scheme.

23,000+ US victims · $1.7B in bitcoin · $1.7B restitution ordered (Sept 2023)
the tell

Your money sits in their wallet, not in an exchange account in your name, and returns arrive as numbers on their dashboard.

source: CFTC complaint (PYMNTS; BehindMLM)
S2//The bot that robs its owner
the pitch

YouTube tutorials, often with AI avatars and voices on aged channels, show you how to deploy a 'front-running MEV bot' in Remix, fund it, and press Start().

what was actually happening

The contract hides an attacker address (XOR scrambling, decimal-to-hex tricks) and sends your ETH to it. Fallback paths let the attacker withdraw even if you never press Start. Compare jared's real bot: owner-only, private, and it nets almost nothing per sandwich.

Top attacker address: 244.9 ETH (~$902K) · running since early 2024
the tell

You're told to run code you can't read and send it money. Real MEV bots aren't handed out on YouTube.

source: SentinelLABS (SentinelOne); Radom
S3//AI washing
the pitch

Investment advisers marketed AI-driven predictions to win clients.

what was actually happening

The SEC found Delphia's claimed AI wasn't backed by an actual algorithm, and Global Predictions called itself the "first regulated AI financial advisor" without basis. First-ever AI-washing cases.

$400K combined penalties (Delphia $225K, Global Predictions $175K), March 2024
the tell

Ask what data the model uses and what it has done live on data it never saw. 'AI' with no answer is marketing.

source: SEC settled orders (Morrison Foerster)
S4//The keys to your account
the pitch

Bot platforms need exchange API keys to trade for you.

what was actually happening

3Commas called reports of leaked keys false rumours in Oct 2022, then confirmed the breach on 29 Dec 2022. Attackers placed unauthorised trades on Binance, KuCoin and Coinbase.

~100,000 API keys taken · 10,000 published · reported losses $20M+
the tell

A bot never needs withdrawal permission. Lock keys to an IP and use a sub-account.

source: HAPI Labs via ForkLog; Bitdefender
S5//The 95% win-rate robot
the pitch

A forex EA with a smooth equity curve and a near-perfect win rate.

what was actually happening

Grid and martingale robots add to losing trades until the price comes back, banking small wins. The rare streak that doesn't come back wipes the account. MQL5's own store filter lists Martingale and Grid as product types.

Top MQL5 signal: 76.8% wins, average loss 1.8× average win, 53% drawdown, site forecast −100%
the tell

High win rate with a large drawdown, or average losses much bigger than average wins.

source: MQL5 capture (this dossier)try it
S6//The perfect backtest
the pitch

"Backtested over 10 years: +4,000%."

what was actually happening

Try enough settings on the same history and one will look brilliant by luck. Holly's own description is a nightly loop of backtest, tune, and keep the winners.

See the overfitting lab: best of 300 random strategies on a pure coin-flip market
the tell

No live track record on data the strategy never saw, or results that never include costs.

source: Trade Ideas capture (this dossier)try it
S7//Reviews that are ads
the pitch

"Top 7 AI trading bots of 2026" lists and influencer screenshots.

what was actually happening

Bot platforms pay referrers a share of fees, three levels deep in Axiom's case. Many comparison pages are affiliate pages. Leaderboards show survivors: 19 of the top 48 MQL5 signals have zero subscribers, and blown accounts disappear.

Axiom router carries referralLevel2Address and referralLevel3Address
the tell

Every link has a referral code, and nobody posts the losers.

source: Axiom bundle (this dossier)
S8//Scarcity and hype pricing
the pitch

"Only a few copies left at current price!" "Strictly limited run of only 5 copies."

what was actually happening

Software has no copies to run out of. The CFTC's Jan 2024 advisory, "AI Won't Turn Trading Bots into Money Machines", warns about promised returns of tens of thousands of percent and 100% win rates.

Top EA listing: $949, 'price will soon increase to $999'
the tell

Urgency, guaranteed returns, and the question nobody answers: if it works, why is it for sale?

source: MQL5 capture (this dossier); CFTC advisory (Finextra)
