synopsis

One-page summary

rugs.fun ships a React SPA whose provablyFair machinery is completely client-visible. The generatePrices(serverSeed, roundId, externalHash?) function — the entire Rug Pull RNG — lives in generatePrices-Cb6Po-5f.js, 16 LoC after unminify. Every tuning constant is exposed: RUG_PROB=0.005, RUG_IMPACT=0.98, GOD_CANDLE_CHANCE=1e-5. The server broadcasts a 17-event socket surface including shortLiquidation, sidebetResult, rugpoolDrawing, and rugpoolWin — confirming long + short + sidebet + jackpot markets. The commit-reveal fairness scheme is NOT anchored on-chain; it lives in server WebSocket frames alone. 10 attack vectors + 8 commit-reveal bypasses are catalogued, with a 6-strategy operator playbook ranging from legitimate god-candle EV farming to the ambiguous recovered-seed short.
