6 strategies — ordered by legitimacy

LEGITIMATEP1God-candle farming — the +2% EV hold strategy

Enter standard-game rounds at tick 0 with a small position (minimizes exposure to early rug). HOLD through drawdowns while u ≤ 100 (base multiplier scale). Each tick while u ≤ 100 contributes +1e-4 EV from the god-candle branch alone. Over ~200 expected ticks, that's +2% expected value stacked on top of the base game.

requires

Patience to sit through losing streaks. Bankroll large enough to absorb ~5-10 losing rounds in a row.

expected uplift

+1.5% to +2.5% EV uplift vs. aggressive cash-out strategies. House edge still negative overall (~1-3%) but god-candle farming halves it.

legality

100% within the published rules. The algorithm is handed to you by the client; using it strategically is table-stakes gameplay.

LEGITIMATEP2Rakeback-crate EV cycling

XP accrues from wagering. Daily rakeback crates have known SOL ranges (Iron 0.001-0.02, Bronze 0.005-1, higher tiers up to 20 SOL). Compute the uniform-expected value per crate type at your level, then wager just enough SOL per day to unlock the daily crate without exceeding where the crate EV + rakeback exceeds additional wager EV.

requires

Account at level 10+ (Bronze unlock). Track daily crate claim window. Wager in cheapest game-mode by variance (likely honeypot or Rug Pull with god-candle holds).

expected uplift

Crate EV at Bronze ≈ 0.5 SOL average per daily claim. If daily wager cost to unlock is 10 SOL at 2% house edge = 0.2 SOL cost. Net +0.3 SOL/day uplift. Scales up with tier.

legality

Legitimate rakeback optimization. The system is designed to encourage this to drive engagement.

LEGITIMATEP3Sidebet skew — reverse-engineering published odds

Sidebets have maxWinChance filtering. By probing bet-size × target combinations in the UI and reading the client-side `R4(target, coin)` function output, you can enumerate the exact house edge per sidebet variant. Pick the lowest-house-edge sidebet variants and only play those.

requires

Patience to probe sidebet UI. Browser devtools to read computed win-chance values.

expected uplift

Reducing avg house edge from ~2.5% to ~1% on sidebet-only play. Over 1000 SOL wagered, that's +15 SOL vs. uninformed play.

legality

Trivially legitimate — you're just reading the public odds the client computes.

AMBIGUOUSP4Short-side positioning on recovered price curves

If attack vector A1 or A4 pans out (serverSeed obtainable mid-round via WS frames OR Alea PRNG state recoverable from first ~15 ticks), you can enter a MAX-leverage short at the exact tick before the recovered rug tick. Payoff: ~50x on leverage, 98% price drop on rug. Risk: zero if the recovery is correct.

requires

Verified A1 or A4 — needs live WS capture + PRNG state-recovery PoC. Both are hypotheses as of this dossier.

expected uplift

If viable: +4900% per executed round. Operator will patch within hours of detection.

legality

Gray — the ToS likely prohibits 'exploiting bugs or vulnerabilities.' Using recovered-but-public algorithm against the operator falls between skill play and bug exploitation. Jurisdiction-dependent.

AMBIGUOUSP5UUID-v7 round-collision tracking

If attack vector A2 pans out (operator reuses serverSeed across v7-roundId rounds), build a database of (serverSeed → priceArray) pairs from historical reveals. On every new v7 round, check if commit matches a known-reveal entry → you know the full price curve before the first tick.

requires

Verified A2 — needs ~500 historical rounds analyzed for seed/hash collisions. Trivial to compute if reveals are public.

expected uplift

If viable: perfect prediction on any colliding round. Rare but recognizable.

legality

Same as P4.

DEFENSIVEP6Audit — commit-reveal verification pipeline

Build a bot that: (a) subscribes to every round-open event, (b) records commitHash with external timestamp (hits a trusted timestamping service on each commit), (c) on reveal, verifies hash and records the pair, (d) runs V2-detection (correlate house win rate vs. pool skew at commit time). Publishes findings publicly as commit-reveal integrity report.

requires

WS client. External timestamping (OTS). Statistical pipeline. Public dashboard.

expected uplift

No direct profit, but severe reputational leverage if V2 or similar can be shown operating in the wild.

legality

Public-interest security research. The dossier approach.
